Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in the mobile application "UGR8" (the "App") for users in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand.

Summary of Data Practices

We collect your birthday, precise geolocation, and Bluetooth/local network access solely to provide localized, personalized insights and peer-to-peer functionality within the App. This data is stored exclusively on your device; we do not operate servers, backends, or databases, and we do not upload, sync, or retain your data on any infrastructure under our control. We do not link this data to your identity, do not use it for tracking, and do not share it with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

A. Birthday Information

We collect your birthday to calculate and display personalized insights inside the App.

B. Precise Geolocation

We collect your precise geolocation (latitude and longitude) only when the App is in active use to perform location-based calculations (including spherical coordinate modeling and localized energy field mapping) that constitute the App's core functionality. We do not collect geolocation in the background, do not maintain a location history, and do not track your movements over time.

C. Bluetooth and Local Network

The App requests access to Bluetooth and your local network solely to enable direct peer-to-peer communication between your device and other nearby devices for core App functionality (e.g., device discovery, connection handshake, and localized data exchange). We do not use these permissions to collect, store, or transmit any personal information, device identifiers, connection logs, or communication content to ourselves or any third party. All data exchanged over these connections remains on the participating devices unless you independently enable iCloud backup. Bluetooth and network access are not used for location tracking, behavioral profiling, or advertising.

D. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or process your name, email address, physical address, payment information, health data, contacts, photos, microphone input, calendar, or any user-generated content. The App does not currently integrate any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics, Meta SDK).

2. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your information based on:

  • Consent: You provide your birthday and grant location, Bluetooth, and local network permissions voluntarily when using the App; and
  • Contractual Necessity: Processing is necessary to provide the App's core features to you.

If you are in a jurisdiction that recognizes additional legal bases (such as "legitimate interests"), our legitimate interest is to operate and improve the App. You may withdraw consent for location, Bluetooth, or local network access at any time through your iOS Settings; however, withdrawal will disable the App's core features that depend on those permissions. You may withdraw consent for birthday data by deleting it in App Settings or uninstalling the App.

3. How We Use Information

We use your birthday, precise geolocation, and Bluetooth/local network access solely to provide the App's core functionality: generating localized, personalized insights and enabling direct device-to-device communication. No other use is made, including for advertising, marketing, profiling, or tracking. We do not use your data to train any algorithm or artificial intelligence model.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose your information to any third parties for commercial purposes. Your data is stored locally on your iOS device; we do not maintain any server copy, backend database, or cloud infrastructure under our control.

If you enable iCloud backup, your local device data (including App data) may be backed up by Apple to data centers located in the United States, Europe, or other regions. Apple is an independent data controller for iCloud services. We do not control, operate, or assume any liability for Apple's iCloud infrastructure, data practices, or security measures. Please review Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

5. Data Retention

We retain your birthday, geolocation, and Bluetooth/network information only as long as the App remains installed on your device and you have not manually deleted it. Because data is stored locally and we do not maintain server copies:

  • Uninstalling the App will delete the data from your device;
  • Editing your information in App Settings overwrites the previous data, effectively deleting the old version;
  • If you require confirmation of deletion, uninstall the App.

6. Data Editing & Deletion

You may edit your birthday or permissions at any time by navigating to App → Settings or your iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. You may delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the App. If you have enabled iCloud backup, you may also need to manage or delete backups through your iCloud settings.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information, which may include accessing, correcting, deleting, or restricting use of your data. Because we store data only locally on your device, the most effective way to exercise these rights is through the App's Settings, iOS system permissions, or by uninstalling the App. If you require assistance, contact us at Bio-Cosmo@outlook.com.

  • United States: Depending on your state of residence, you may have additional rights such as the right to know, delete, or opt out of the sale/sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information.
  • United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand: Nothing in this Policy limits your statutory rights under applicable local privacy and consumer protection laws.

Your data is stored exclusively on your device using the iOS operating system's built-in security features. We do not operate servers, cloud infrastructure, or backend systems that store your data. Consequently, we do not and cannot provide independent security guarantees beyond those offered by your device and iOS.

You acknowledge that the security of your data depends entirely on:

  • Apple's iOS security architecture and encryption;
  • Whether you have enabled a device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID;

Because we do not collect or store your data on our own servers, the risk of a centralized data breach by us is negligible. Should we become aware of any event that may have compromised your personal information, we will take steps consistent with applicable legal requirements in your jurisdiction. We have no independent obligation to monitor for, investigate, or notify you of breaches occurring on your device or within Apple's iCloud infrastructure.
